The Problem

The carbon credit market
is built on broken promises.

Every year, companies pay billions for carbon credits to "offset" their pollution — paying someone else to plant trees or build solar farms instead of cutting their own emissions.

Independent research shows most credits overstate their impact by 2–5×. Forests that were never at risk. Trees that were already there. Numbers that were simply made up.

"That's greenwashing — paying for impact that doesn't exist."

Based on independent analyses of Verra-certified REDD+ projects
(Science, 2023 · The Guardian, 2023)
